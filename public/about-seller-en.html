Start selling

Go from registration to running your store in four steps.

Seller Terms

  1. Create your account

    Open the seller portal and choose to create an account. Enter your details and review the Seller Terms and Privacy Policy.

  2. Set up your store

    Complete your store profile, business details and any requested verification. Some accounts or products may need approval before selling.

  3. List your products

    Add clear descriptions, photos, variants, prices and stock quantities. Check the destinations each item can serve before listing.

  4. Manage orders and payouts

    Follow orders and tracking in the seller dashboard. Review commissions, balances and payout conditions in your account.

Connect your external store

Start by checking your platform and target destinations. Integration scope depends on your account and approval by the partnerships team.

Ask about store integration

  1. Tell us about your store

    Share your store URL, platform and target markets. Explain whether you need catalogue, order or tracking integration.

  2. Test the integration

    Once compatibility is confirmed, agree on sandbox access. Test product, eligibility and price sync, then submit a test order and check shipment updates.

  3. Prepare for launch

    Agree on responsibilities, support and costs before activating the integration. Monitor sync and tracking errors after launch.

Affiliate marketing

For creators and publishers. Enquire about joining. Programme availability and terms are subject to review by the team.

Ask about affiliate marketing

  1. Tell us about your channels

    Share your channel links, content format and audience.

  2. Ask about attribution

    If the programme is available and you are accepted, confirm how to get a referral link and what qualifies as an eligible order before sharing it.

  3. Check the commission terms

    Ask about rates, payout timing and how returns or cancellations are handled. Disclose affiliate links to your audience.
