3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the service and fulfil orders, including inspection and international delivery. Basis: performance of a contract with you.
  • To keep accounting and tax records. Basis: legal obligation.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent fraud. Basis: our legitimate interest in a safe service.
  • To answer your messages and reports. Basis: contract or legitimate interest.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who receives it

  • Render (hosting), which stores the platform's data on our behalf.
  • Stripe (payments), for customers who pay by card.
  • Shipping carriers, which receive your name, delivery address and phone number to deliver your parcel.
  • Taobao / Alibaba: for sellers, the authorization they give to their own account. Customers' personal data is never sent to Taobao or to Taobao merchants; their orders are shipped to Kinan's warehouse address.
  • Sellers see the products, quantities and prices of their orders, but not the customer's name, address or phone number.
  • Google and Facebook, if you choose “Continue with Google” or “Continue with Facebook”: they confirm who you are and give us your name, email address and profile picture. We do not post anything or read your contacts.
  • Our SMS provider (Twilio or Brevo), if you sign in with your phone number: it receives your number to send you a one-time code.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some providers, including our hosting provider, process data outside the European Economic Area, and orders are handled at our warehouse in China. Where data leaves the EEA we rely on safeguards permitted by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the transfer being necessary to perform your contract (for example, delivering your order).

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account is open, then deleted unless we must keep it.
  • Orders and payments: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Seller verification data: as long as the shop is active, and for six months after it closes.
  • Security logs: for a limited period set by our hosting provider.

7. Cookies and device storage

We use one essential cookie, sid, to keep you signed in. Your cart and last delivery address are saved only in your own browser so you don't lose them. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and our fonts are hosted on our own server.
